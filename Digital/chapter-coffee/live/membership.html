GOOD TO KNOW

A few little
questions.

Everything you need to know about making yourself at home.

Is Reading Club membership free? +
Yes. The proposed Reading Club program is complimentary to join, with no paid membership tiers.
Can I read books without being a member? +
Absolutely. Everyone is welcome to browse and read books for free while visiting Chapter & Coffee.
How do I earn loyalty points? +
Under the proposed program, members earn 10 points per eligible dollar spent at the café and 25 bonus points per eligible book rental.
Can I take books home? +
Yes. Books can be enjoyed freely on-site, and selected titles can be rented for reading at home. Rental availability and terms would be confirmed by the café.
Can I redeem points online? +
Online redemption is not part of this demonstration. The final redemption process and available rewards would be established when the loyalty program is implemented.
